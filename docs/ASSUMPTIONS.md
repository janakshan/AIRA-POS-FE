# Prototype assumptions (for client review)

Where `SOURCES/` is silent, the prototype makes these choices. Each line: ID · phase · assumption · why.

## Cross-cutting

- **A-001 · P1** · A sensitive action needs a fresh PIN verification (valid 2 minutes, usable once) from an employee whose role holds the permission; the logged-in user's own permissions are not enough. · REQ-184…232, ADR-010: the device stays logged in; the person acting is identified by PIN.
- **A-002 · P1** · Employees without a linked user account (e.g. Priya, bakery cashier) have no permissions for PIN-protected actions. · No employee-level permission model is specified.
- **A-003 · P1** · Default reasons are the REQ-275…285 list plus "Price correction"; "Other" requires a comment of at least 3 characters. · Reasons are admin-configurable but no admin screen is in P1.
- **A-004 · P1** · Every catalog write is audited (who, device, location, before/after); only PIN-protected writes carry an employee and reason. · INS-212…229.
- **A-005 · P1** · A new error code `VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` (HTTP 428) is returned when a write needs a PIN verification that is missing, expired or already used. · Not in the error-code list.
- **A-296 · DASH-002** · The catalog names a "Location Dashboard" but doesn't define it. It is a "today across my locations" view at `/dashboard/locations`: totals plus one card per location the user can see (sales, orders, average order; open tables and pending KOTs for restaurants; low stock with `inventory.view`; the hourly curve), busiest first, refreshed every minute. "Open dashboard" switches to that location and goes to DASH-001. It needs `report.sales.view` (cross-location takings are manager-level), and the link only appears for users with more than one location. Each row is the same simulated figure as DASH-001 for that location (A-290). New endpoint `GET /dashboard/locations`. · DASH-001 only ever shows the current location; the source requires branches to run on one central system.

## Catalog (P1)

- **A-101** · Categories are an unlimited-depth tree via `parentId`; deactivating is blocked while a category has active subcategories or products (409). Nothing is deleted. · REQ-114…136 ("not limited to two levels").
- **A-102** · Category and product codes are unique per tenant, case-insensitive. Product barcodes are also unique per tenant (across all products), and a product can't list the same barcode twice. · Codes appear on reports and receipts; a POS scan must resolve to one product.
- **A-103** · Products are tenant-wide; each location has its own row for "sold here", "available now", a stock note and a price override. Switching a product off keeps the row (and its price). · REQ-153…180.
- **A-104** · Location price = override, else base price. Lowering any effective selling price (base or location) counts as a price override: `pos.price.override` + manager PIN + reason. Raising prices needs only `catalog.manage`. · REQ-224 mentions "change price" without direction.
- **A-105** · Quick Pad layouts resolve Device → Location → default (INS-297…321). A device's own layout is optional; "Use location layout" removes it. Platform/tenant-level layouts are not modelled. Devices are registered on SET-005 (A-304). · Location and device are the levels staff actually change.
- **A-106** · The POS shows a category's own products next to its subcategories when drilled in (e.g. Rice & Curry → [Rice & Curry, Fried Rice, Biriyani]). · Mixed levels exist in the requirement example.
- **A-107** · Products and categories have optional Tamil and Sinhala names; the UI shows the name in the user's language and falls back to English. Seeded translations are demo text and need native-speaker review (P6). · REQ-861…883.
- **A-108** · Pricing is in the tenant currency only. · Multi-currency is not in the requirements.
- **A-110** · Button images are optional per product/category, downscaled to ≤256 px in the browser and stored as a data URL in the mock (the real API will store the upload and return a URL). No icon library. · No asset storage in the prototype.
- **A-111** · Per location, each product has a kitchen station (which KOT printer/display it goes to, or none for ready-to-sell items) and a "service charge applies" flag. Stations are seeded (Main Kitchen, Beverage Bar, Bakery Counter); stations are managed on SET-008 (A-306). Stock stays a free-text note until P4 inventory. · REQ-153…180, REQ-354…361, REQ-463…483.
- **A-109** · New endpoints not in `07_MOCK_API_CONTRACT.md`: `PATCH /categories/:id`, `GET /categories?tree=true`, `PUT /location-products/:productId`, `GET|PUT /price-matrix`, `GET|PUT|DELETE /quick-pad-layout` (with optional `deviceId`), `GET /kitchen-stations`, `GET /devices`, `GET /reasons`, `GET /audit-events`. · Needed for CAT-002/005/006/007 and the audit trail.

## Retail POS (P2)

- **A-201 · POS-001** · The sale is a local draft on the device (per tenant, location and terminal) until Pay/Hold creates the server order. It survives a reload. · Speed on busy counters and tolerance of flaky connections.
- **A-202 · POS-001** · Changing quantity or removing a line in an unsaved draft needs no PIN; the REQ-217…232 PIN rules apply once the order exists (after Hold/Pay or a KOT is sent). · Nothing has been committed or sent to the kitchen yet.
- **A-203 · POS-001** · Totals: service charge = location rate × lines flagged "service charge" (CAT-005); tax = location rate × tax-EXCLUSIVE lines only (INCLUSIVE prices already contain tax); rounding half-up once per total. Seed rates: Main Restaurant 10 % service; all locations 18 % VAT. · No rates or rounding rules in the source docs.
- **A-204 · POS-001** · Prices in the cart follow the live location price until checkout; a line whose product is switched off or marked unavailable is flagged and blocks payment. · Avoids charging stale prices.
- **A-205 · POS-002** · A barcode scanner is treated as a keyboard (fast burst ending in Enter). Search matches name, Tamil/Sinhala name, code and barcode of what the location sells. · Standard USB/Bluetooth scanner behaviour.
- **A-206 · POS-001** · `GET /pos-settings` is added to the contract for per-location tax/service rates. Tax, discount cap, return window and receipt footer are edited on SET-002, the service rate on SET-007 (A-297, A-298). · Needed for totals.
- **A-207 · POS-002** · Full product search is a dialog over the POS (F2 / Search button) that stays open for several additions; "3*S01" or "3 x kottu" adds a quantity. Inline search stays for quick one-offs. · Speed and minimal typing (UI design system).
- **A-208 · POS-003** · Customers are identified by phone, stored in E.164; Sri Lankan numbers are accepted as 077…, 77…, +94… One phone number belongs to one customer per tenant (409 on duplicates, pointing at the owner). · REQ-415…419 ("search by phone; if found auto-fill; if not, quick create").
- **A-209 · POS-003** · Quick create needs only name + phone (optional type); address and other details are completed later in CUS-002. Anyone who can sell (`pos.sale.create`, including waiters taking phone and delivery orders) or has `customer.manage` can quick-create a customer. Editing customer details needs `customer.manage`. · Keeps the counter fast (revised in P3: waiters take delivery orders).
- **A-210 · POS-003** · The customer is attached to the draft sale (walk-in by default) and cleared with it; the cart shows the customer's live outstanding balance. · REQ-399…419.
- **A-211 · POS-004/005** · Because the sale is a local draft, every discount/charge is first approved by the server (`POST /pos/adjustments`), which checks PIN + permission + reason and writes the audit event; the draft stores the approved record and the order (POS-008) will reference it. Removing one voids it (`POST /pos/adjustments/:id/void`) — never deleted — and clearing a sale voids its adjustments with the system reason "Sale cleared". · Makes draft-stage changes auditable (INS-212…229).
- **A-212 · POS-004** · Every discount (percentage, amount, promotion; bill or item) needs `pos.discount.apply` + employee PIN + reason (REQ-222). Percentage discounts are capped per location (seed 50 %). Promotions come from the admin list; their value is taken from the server. Removing a discount needs no PIN (price goes up).
- **A-213 · POS-005** · Charge types are configured per location (Main: service 10 % automatic, delivery Rs 250, packaging Rs 50, other); edited on SET-007, A-298). Adding a charge at its default needs no PIN; a custom amount, an "other" charge, waiving/changing the service charge, or removing a charge needs `pos.charge.manage` (new permission; managers/owners) + PIN + reason. · "Authorised staff can add, remove or modify" (REQ-463…483).
- **A-214 · POS-004/005** · Totals order: item discounts → bill discounts (spread across lines by value, exact to the cent) → service charge on discounted service-charge lines → other charges (no tax on charges) → tax on discounted tax-exclusive lines. · No rules in the source docs.
- **A-215 · POS-006/007** · One shared policy (`SENSITIVE_ACTIONS` in `@rbp/types`) lists every sensitive action with its permission; the POS dialogs and the API both read it. A PIN is verified **for one action**: the server checks the employee's permission at PIN time (a correct PIN without permission is refused immediately and doesn't count toward lockout) and the verification can only be used for that action, once, within 2 minutes.
- **A-216 · POS-007** · Reasons are tenant-configured per action (`appliesTo`); e.g. drawer reasons (Change for customer, Cash pickup, Shift count) are only offered when opening the drawer. "Manager instruction" and "Other" apply everywhere; "Other" needs a comment.
- **A-217 · POS-006** · Opening the cash drawer outside a sale needs a PIN (cashiers may approve it themselves) and a reason, and is audited.
- **A-218 · POS-001** · Staff can change one item's price on a sale (`pos.price.override`, manager PIN + reason, before → after audited). Restoring the catalog price needs no PIN when it raises the price back.
- **A-219 · REP-006** · The audit log is read-only and append-only; events keep the location/device names of the time. Draft-sale quantity reductions and removals stay free (your decision); decrease/remove/cancel/return/refund are enforced by `requireVerifiedAction(ctx, verification, actionCode)` when saved orders and payments arrive (POS-008/010/011).
- **A-220 · POS-008** · A draft becomes a server **order** at Hold or Pay. The server re-prices lines from the live location price, attaches the approved adjustments (each to one order only) and computes totals with the shared `computeTotals`. Invoice numbers are per location (`MAIN-000001`), returns `RTN-MAIN-000001`.
- **A-221 · POS-008** · One payment method per sale (split payment is future, REQ-903): cash (tendered ≥ total, change shown), card (simulated terminal approval code), bank transfer (reference required) or credit sale (needs a customer; adds to their balance). No QR payment — not in the requirements.
- **A-222 · POS-009** The printer is the location's receipt printer, named on SET-008 (A-306). · Receipts come from a server receipt model; printing goes to a simulated receipt printer (and the browser print dialog, 80 mm). The first print is ORIGINAL, later ones REPRINT (watermarked, audited separately).
- **A-223 · POS-010** · Held sales can be resumed on one terminal at a time. Once saved, reducing or removing an item needs a manager PIN + reason (`pos.item.quantity.decrease` / `pos.item.remove`), recorded as a cancelled quantity; additions stay free. Resuming requires an empty current sale.
- **A-224 · POS-011** · Returns: within 30 days (per-location setting), up to the quantity not yet returned. Refund = the units' share of the line's net after discounts + their share of service charge and tax; delivery/packaging/other charges are refunded only when everything is returned. Refund to cash or the original method (credit sales reduce the customer's balance). Stock return comes with P4 inventory.
- **A-225 · POS-012** · Cancel = unpaid (open/held) order, manager PIN + reason; its discounts/charges are voided. Void = a paid invoice from **today** with no returns, manager PIN + reason (`pos.invoice.void`), refunds every payment. Older or partly returned sales use Return. Cancelled/voided orders stay in history with who approved it and why.
- **A-291 · SAL-001** · Sales → Orders is assigned screen ID SAL-001 (none in the screen catalog). It lists every saved order at the **current** location, newest first, opening on today; filters are status, order type, payment method and a date range (today / 7 days / 30 days / custom / all time), all kept in the URL. Anyone who can sell (`pos.sale.create`) can open it. · IA lists "Orders" under Sales with no screen spec.
- **A-292 · SAL-001** · `GET /orders` gains `to` (ISO, inclusive), `paymentMethod` (orders with a SALE payment of that method) and now honours `type` for the general list. · Needed for the SAL-001 filters.
- **A-293 · SAL-001** · The order page reuses the POS receipt (reprint), return and void/cancel dialogs with the same PIN + reason rules (A-222, A-224, A-225): actions are offered to everyone and a manager's PIN approves them, as on the POS. Cancelling an order with items already sent to the kitchen stays on the POS, which asks what happens to the food (A-230). · One set of rules wherever the action starts.

### P3 Restaurant / KOT

- **A-226 · REST-002** · Restaurant ordering uses the same POS screen with an order type (Dine-in / Takeaway / Delivery), shown at RESTAURANT/MIXED locations when `POS_RESTAURANT` is on. A counter sale there defaults to Takeaway. The type is fixed once the order is saved. Other locations sell RETAIL. · Your decision ("Same POS screen + order type").
- **A-227 · REST-001** · Tables are a floor grid grouped by area (seed Main: Indoor T1–T8, Outdoor O1–O4, Bar B1–B3). A table's state is derived from its open/held order: Free, Occupied, or Bill printed. One open order per table. Dine-in needs a free table. · Your decision ("Floor grid by area").
- **A-228 · KOT-001** · Send to kitchen sends each line's unsent quantity as one KOT per kitchen station (the station comes from CAT-005). Items with no station, such as bakery and short eats, are marked sent without a ticket. Every later round makes a new KOT. Numbers are per location (`KOT-MAIN-000001`). · REQ-379…395.
- **A-229 · KOT-003** · KOT lifecycle: New → Preparing → Ready → Completed (served or picked up). Only `kot.manage` can move tickets; waiters see the board read-only. The board polls every 5 s (a real backend would push). Tickets waiting 15 min or more are flagged late. · FLOW-KOT-001; no timing rule in the source docs.
- **A-230 · SCN-004** · Cancelling units the kitchen already has (unsent units are cancelled first) needs a disposition: Not prepared, Resell, Wastage or Staff meal, then the usual manager PIN and reason. The kitchen gets a red CANCEL ticket, which is acknowledged straight from New. Wastage feeds P4 inventory later.
- **A-231 · REST-004** · Moving a saved order to another free table needs `restaurant.table.transfer` (manager PIN) and a reason (new reason "Customer request"). Its open kitchen tickets follow it to the new table.
- **A-232 · REST-007** · Print bill marks the table "Bill printed" and shows a pro-forma BILL (not paid) that doesn't count as the receipt's first print. Paying, cancelling or voiding the order frees the table.
- **A-233 · REST-002** · Leave table saves the order and frees this terminal, and the order stays open on the table. Tapping an occupied table resumes its order, if no other terminal has it open.
- **A-234 · REST-005/006** · Paying a Takeaway or Delivery order sends anything not yet sent to the kitchen, so no paid food is missed. Delivery needs an address and phone; its status moves forward only. Sending makes it Preparing, and it becomes Ready once all its kitchen tickets are ready. Staff move it to Out for delivery and Delivered from sales history. Rider assignment is future (DEL-001, P5).
- **A-235 · REST-002** · Item notes (special instructions, up to 120 characters, with quick chips) print on the KOT.
- **A-236 · REST-006** · The delivery form asks for the phone first and looks the customer up by it (any common format). For a known customer it fills their saved delivery address, falling back to their address, and attaches them to the sale. If they have several addresses, they're offered as chips. An address staff already typed is never overwritten. A new number is typed in by hand. Saving a delivery order for a customer remembers its address as their delivery address for next time. · REQ-415…419 ("search by phone; if found auto-fill").

### P4 Customer

- **A-237 · CUS-001** · Customers belong to the tenant, not a location. Balances, stats and order history cover every location. The list searches by name or any phone number (any format), filters by type and "owes money", and sorts by name, recent order or balance. A full phone number with no match offers to create the customer with it. · REQ-399…419.
- **A-238 · CUS-002** · A customer has 1–5 phone numbers, each with a label, and exactly one is primary. Each number belongs to only one customer per tenant: a clash names and links the owner. Blank address, delivery address or notes clears the field. "Deliver to the same address" leaves the delivery address empty, and deliveries then use the address. The full form needs `customer.manage`; POS quick create stays open to sellers (A-209).
- **A-239 · CUS-003/004** · Stats count paid orders only (voided excluded): total spent is net of returns; average and last order are also shown. Order history lists every order at every location, newest first. It opens a read-only order view, because receipts and reprints stay on the POS of that location.
- **A-240 · CUS-005** · The balance is a statement built from the records: opening balance, credit sales, credit returns and voids (from the orders themselves), and payments received. Each line shows a running balance, and the final balance equals the customer's outstanding amount. Seeded balances that aren't explained by seeded credit sales are shown as "Brought forward (previous system)".
- **A-241 · CUS-005** · Payments on account (cash, card, or bank transfer with a reference) can be taken by whoever handles the cash drawer (`pos.drawer.open`: cashier, manager, owner) or has `customer.manage`. The amount can't exceed what is owed. Payments are numbered `PAY-000001` per tenant, recorded against the current location, and audited as `customer.payment`. Waiters can view a balance but not take money. Credit limits come with wholesale customers (P5).
- **A-242 · Seed** · Demo customers have past orders numbered `OLD-…`, dated before today at Main and Bakery. Today's sales history and new invoice numbers are unaffected. Every paid sale now updates the customer's last order date.

### P4 Inventory

- **A-243 · INV-*** · Stock is a ledger: on hand is always the sum of an item's movements at a location, and there is no stored quantity. Movement types: OPENING, SALE, RETURN, TRANSFER_OUT, TRANSFER_IN, WASTAGE, STAFF_MEAL, ADJUSTMENT. PURCHASE is posted by goods receiving (A-250) and PRODUCTION_CONSUMPTION by recipes (A-257); PRODUCTION_OUTPUT is reserved for bakery production. Each movement keeps its reference (invoice, return, void, transfer, adjustment, cancelled KOT), who did it, who approved it and why. · 00_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS → Inventory.
- **A-244 · INV-*** · Every sold product is tracked (your decision), counted in whole units: portions for dishes, pieces for short eats, bakery and drinks, packs for grocery. Stock-only ingredients and recipe deduction arrived with RECIPES (A-255…259); dishes with a recipe are made to order. A location also tracks anything that ever moved there, e.g. Central Store holds stock it doesn't sell.
- **A-245 · POS** · Stock leaves at payment (SALE). A saved or sent but unpaid order doesn't hold stock. At 0 on hand an item can't be sold and there is no override (your decision). Tiles show "Out of stock" or "3 left" (when low). The cart can't exceed what's on hand. The server re-checks on save and at payment (409 OUT_OF_STOCK), because another terminal may have sold the last one.
- **A-246 · POS-011/012** · A return puts the goods back in stock by default. Unticking "Put returned items back in stock" records a RETURN followed by WASTAGE, so the ledger still shows what came back. A void puts the whole sale back (RETURN, reference VOID). Cancelled food the kitchen had made (SCN-004) leaves stock as WASTAGE or STAFF_MEAL; nothing moves for Not prepared, and Resell puts the plate in the prepared queue (A-259). Cancelling a whole order doesn't ask for a disposition yet.
- **A-247 · INV-004** · Adjustments follow FLOW-INV-001: item → kind (Count / Add / Remove / Wastage / Staff meal) → quantity → PIN (`inventory.adjust`: manager, owner) → reason → movement → audit. A count records the difference. Stock can't go below 0. Numbered `ADJ-000001` per tenant. Stock reasons: Stock count difference, Expired, Spoiled, Found / miscount, Staff meal, Damaged.
- **A-248 · INV-005** · Transfers are Dispatch → Receive (your decision). Dispatch needs `inventory.transfer` and access to the sender; stock leaves at dispatch (TRANSFER_OUT). The receiver confirms quantities (TRANSFER_IN). A shortage is recorded on the transfer and in the audit, and isn't added back anywhere (lost in transit). The sender can cancel while in transit, which returns the stock. Numbered `TRF-000001`. The destination list shows the user's own locations.
- **A-249 · INV-006** · Minimum stock is set per item and location (`inventory.adjust`, no PIN, audited). At or below the minimum it's LOW, and at 0 it's OUT. Low stock shows on INV-006 (with "Transfer in", prefilled from the location holding the most), as a dashboard alert, and on POS tiles. SMS / email / WhatsApp alerts are future. Seeded demo states: Main has Fish Rice & Curry 3 and Egg Pastry 2 (low) and Vegetable Roti 0 (out); Bakery has Sandwich Bread 24 of 30 (low), with Central Store holding 60.
- **A-250 · PUR-*** · Purchasing is Supplier → Purchase order → Goods receipt (GRN). Only receiving changes stock: each item received is a PURCHASE movement (reference `GRN-000001`) at the PO's deliver-to location. Placing or cancelling an order doesn't move stock. Everything needs the PURCHASING feature and `purchasing.manage` (owner, manager). Orders and receipts are limited to the user's locations. POs are numbered `PO-000001` and GRNs `GRN-000001`, per tenant. Every write is audited.
- **A-251 · PUR-001/002** · Suppliers belong to the whole tenant, not one location. They are never deleted; deactivating one hides it from new orders, while orders already placed can still be received. Names are unique per tenant, and codes run `SUP-001` upward. Open orders, last order and value received are derived from the orders and GRNs, not stored.
- **A-252 · PUR-003** · A PO starts as DRAFT, which can still be edited, then becomes ORDERED when placed (in one step with "Place order"). Unit cost is entered per line in minor units, because products carry no cost price. The last cost paid for an item is filled in as a suggestion. It can be cancelled, with a reason, only before anything has arrived. There is no approval step, no tax, no supplier price list and no emailing the PO; these are future.
- **A-253 · PUR-004** · A delivery can be part of an order: what isn't received stays outstanding, and the status becomes PARTIALLY_RECEIVED until everything is in (RECEIVED). Receiving more than is outstanding is refused. A partly received PO can't be short-closed yet. There is no returning goods to a supplier and no supplier invoice or payable tracking, only the supplier's invoice number recorded on the GRN.
- **A-254 · PUR seed** · The pilot outlets buy finished short eats and bakery goods from outside suppliers, because stock-only ingredients come with RECIPES (A-244). Seeded data: 5 suppliers (1 inactive) and one PO per status. PO-000004 (Egg Pastry and Vegetable Roti for Main) is ordered and overdue, so receiving it clears two seeded low/out states in a demo. The seeded GRNs add stock only to items the inventory demo states don't depend on.
- **A-255 · REC-*** · **Prototype model, to be finalized after approval.** Kitchen ingredients are stock-only products (`kind: INGREDIENT`) on the same stock ledger, so they are received on purchase orders, counted and adjusted in Inventory, and alerted when low. They never appear on the POS or in the catalog. Each dish has at most one recipe: whole units of each ingredient per serving (portions, pieces or packs, with no decimals or grams yet). Everything needs the RECIPES feature and `production.manage` (owner, manager); REC-005 also allows `kot.manage` (kitchen).
- **A-256 · REC-002/003** · A dish with an active recipe is made to order (your decision). It has no finished stock. "Can make" is what the scarcest ingredient allows at the location, plus prepared plates ready to resell, and the POS shows and blocks it the same way as finished stock (at 0 it can't be sold, no override yet; your decision). The server adds up ingredient needs across all lines, so two dishes sharing chicken can't oversell it (409 OUT_OF_STOCK naming the ingredient). Pausing a recipe puts the dish back on finished-stock tracking. Finished stock recorded before the recipe was added stays in the ledger but is hidden.
- **A-257 · REC stock** · Ingredients leave stock at payment, like sales: PRODUCTION_CONSUMPTION ("Used in recipe") per ingredient, with the invoice as reference. A void puts the ingredients back (RETURN). A refund doesn't, because cooked food never goes back into ingredients. Staff meals from the POS (§22) and bakery production (BAK) come later.
- **A-258 · REC-004** · A portion is described in words, with optional grams and portions per pack; it's information, not a unit conversion. The requirement planner takes expected sales per dish (default: the daily average over the last 30 days), works out ingredient needs against on hand, and suggests what to buy, e.g. "need 80 · have 35 · buy 45+". "Create purchase order" opens PUR-003 prefilled (`?lines=`), using the last cost paid. There is no demand forecasting yet.
- **A-259 · REC-005 / SCN-004** · Food cancelled after the kitchen made it: for a recipe dish, _Wastage_ and _Staff meal_ use its ingredients under that movement type, and _Resell_ uses them (PRODUCTION_CONSUMPTION) and puts the plate in the prepared queue. For other items, Resell queues the plate and it stays in finished stock until sold. The next sale of that item at that location uses queued plates first, oldest first, and records the use on the plate. Plates must be sold within 4 hours by default (adjustable when added by hand). After that they show as Expired, can't be sold, and must be disposed of as Wastage, Staff meal, Disposed or Other, with a reason (audited). Disposing of a recipe plate moves no stock, because its ingredients were already used. Plates can also be added as "made extra".

### P5 Bakery

- **A-260 · BAK-*** · **Prototype model, to be finalized after approval.** The source docs name only the screens, FLOW-BAK-001 and the entities ProductionPlan and ProductionBatch. They give no fields, statuses, endpoints or reason codes, so everything below is assumed. Production uses the same stock ledger as everything else: raw materials leave stock as PRODUCTION_CONSUMPTION, finished goods come in as PRODUCTION_OUTPUT, and rejects and write-offs go out as WASTAGE. Every movement references its batch (`PRODUCTION_BATCH`). It all needs the BAKERY_PRODUCTION feature and `production.manage` (owner, manager).
- **A-261 · BAK locations** · Production runs only at bakery-type locations (Bakery Outlet in the demo). Finished goods are baked into that location's stock and reach the shops through the existing stock transfers (INV-005), e.g. "Bakery Production → Shop A" (§16). There is no separate production store yet.
- **A-262 · BAK formula** · Each bakery product has one production formula: what one batch run uses, in whole units of each raw material (bags, blocks, sachets, eggs — no grams yet), and how many units it yields. Formulas are seeded (bread 20 loaves, each cake 24 slices). Editing them comes later. Raw materials are stock-only ingredients, like REC-001, so they are bought on purchase orders and also appear in the ingredient list (codes M01–M05). Eggs reuse the kitchen's egg item.
- **A-263 · BAK-002 plan** · A plan is one location's production for one date: how many of each product the day needs, rounded up to whole runs (30 slices = 2 runs = 48 expected). Status goes Draft → Confirmed → In progress → Completed, or Draft/Confirmed → Cancelled (with a reason). Only drafts can be edited. Confirming creates one batch per product. After that, the plan's status follows its batches. There can be more than one plan for a day. The plan shows raw materials needed against on hand and can open a prefilled purchase order for what's short.
- **A-264 · BAK-003 batch** · A batch goes Planned → Baking → Completed, or Planned → Cancelled. Anything else is rejected (409). Raw materials leave stock when the batch **starts**. The baker records what was actually used, which defaults to the formula. A batch can't start with more than is on hand (409 OUT_OF_STOCK naming the item), with no override. When output is recorded, everything that came out goes in as PRODUCTION_OUTPUT, then the rejects go out as WASTAGE with a reason, so the ledger shows both. Rejects need no PIN. A started batch can't be cancelled or reversed yet.
- **A-265 · BAK-004/005 wastage** · Wastage reasons come from the tenant reason list, for the new sensitive action `production.wastage`: Burnt, Under-baked, Misshapen, plus the existing Damaged, Expired, Spoiled and Other. Writing off finished goods needs an employee PIN (someone with `production.manage`) and a reason. It creates an ADJ- stock adjustment and is audited. BAK-005 reads every WASTAGE movement of bakery products from the ledger, so INV-004 write-offs appear there too, and totals always match stock.
- **A-266 · BAK-001** · "Produced" means good units from today's completed batches. "Planned" is the sum of today's plans. ### P5 Wholesale

- **A-271 · WHO-003 invoice** · Wholesale prices are a separate tenant price list, VAT inclusive (about 80% of retail in the seed); editing it comes later. - **A-272 · Credit limit** · Going over the credit limit is allowed but warned (your decision). The rep must tick "Continue anyway", and the invoice is flagged "over limit" and audited. There's no PIN override.
- **A-273 · Share/print** · Print uses the 80 mm receipt paper, the same as POS. Share sends the invoice as text: the phone's share sheet where available, a WhatsApp link to the shop's primary number (wa.me), or copy to the clipboard. - **A-274 · Balances** · A shop owes its opening balance plus the unpaid (credit) part of each invoice, less collections and return credits. - **A-275 · WHO-005 returns** · A return needs an employee PIN (`wholesale.return`, anyone with `wholesale.manage`) and a reason (Expired, Damaged, Spoiled, Unsold, Other…). It may be linked to an invoice, and then it can't return more than was invoiced and not yet returned, and is credited at the invoice price, against that invoice first. Otherwise it's credited at today's wholesale price. Each product has one condition per return in the UI.
- **A-276 · WHO-006 routes** · A route is a named list of shops in stop order, with weekdays, a van and a rep's name. ### P5 Reports

- **A-287 · REP sales definition** · The source names the six reports but defines no figures, so these are ours. **Sales** are POS orders that were paid, counted on the local day they were paid. **Refunds** (returns and voids) count on the day the money went back — in every breakdown: by the hour it was refunded, the refunded order's type, the payment method and the employee who refunded it, so each breakdown adds up to net sales even when the order was paid before the period. **Net sales** = what customers paid − refunds. **Orders** = paid orders that weren't voided; average order = net ÷ orders. Cancelled orders were never sales and appear only on REP-005. Gross is before discounts; charges are service charge plus other charges (delivery, packaging); tax is the tax on VAT-exclusive items (VAT-inclusive prices already contain it). Wholesale invoices and staff meals (at sale price) are shown as separate "Other channels" lines, not POS sales (your decision). There's no cost of sales or profit yet (products carry no cost price).
- **A-288 · REP filters and export** · Every report takes a period (presets or custom from–to, local dates, inclusive; default the last 7 days) and one location or all the user can see. The period and location live in the URL. - **A-289 · REP-002…005 details** · REP-002 values each product at its line net after discounts (before service charge and tax), less refunds of that product. REP-003 lists each outlet's POS sales with its share, and vans' wholesale invoices as a separate row (no share). REP-004 splits the ledger for the period into opening, in (received, produced, returned), out (sold, transferred out, wasted, staff meals, used in production or recipes), adjusted (counts, opening stock) and closing; the status is as of now. REP-005 covers discounts and price changes (with amount, approver and reason), items removed from saved orders (from the audit log), cancelled orders, voided invoices and refunds, totalled by reason and by approving employee.
